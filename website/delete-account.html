AI Boxing Coach — account deletion request

You can permanently delete your AI Boxing Coach account and all data associated with it at any time.

Fastest way — in the app

  • Open the app and go to Profile → Account.
  • Tap “Delete account & data” and confirm.

This immediately and permanently removes your account and everything stored for it.

Can't use the app? Request it by email

Email phisoluciones.es@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll verify it's you and delete your account, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login (email / Google sign-in link).
  • Your profile, sessions, rounds and analyses.
  • Uploaded keyframe images and pose data.

Round videos are stored only on your own device and are removed there automatically (about 7 days) or when you delete the app. We may retain minimal records where required by law (e.g. for accounting); these are not used for any other purpose.
