What it sees

It sees the mistakes you miss.

On-device pose estimation and a rule engine read every round and turn it into specific, plain-language coaching — not a score you have to interpret.

01

Guard

Catches when your hands leave the positions that keep you safe — the drop after the cross, the lazy return.

02

Stance & balance

Reads foot position, width and weight so you know when your base is working against you.

03

Punch mechanics

Tracks extension, hip rotation and how the hand recovers — where power comes from and where it leaks.

04

Movement

Reviews head movement and body positioning through the round, not just at the moment you punch.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need special equipment?

No. The app works with your phone camera. Prop it up and train.

Do I need a coach?

No. It's built to give you extra feedback when you train alone — and it complements coached sessions rather than replacing them.

Does it work with a heavy bag?

Yes. Bag work is one of the training environments it's designed for.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes — living room, garage bag or the park all work.

What does the AI analyse?

Today: guard and hand return, stance and balance, punch mechanics and rotation, head movement and footwork — and whether those habits improve over time.

Is this supposed to replace a boxing coach?

No. It gives you more feedback, more often — between coached sessions or when no one's watching.

Is multi-camera support coming?

A dedicated multi-camera system is being explored and tested. Join the hardware beta to follow along.
