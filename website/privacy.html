Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This policy explains what the AI Boxing Coach mobile app and this website (together, the Service) collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The Service is operated by Grant James MacDonald, based in Spain (the “we” in this policy). You can reach us at phisoluciones.es@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Account information

When you create an account we store your email address and authentication details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile from Google — we never receive your Google password.

Training video and images

  • Video of your round is recorded by your phone camera and analysed on your device. The full video is not uploaded — it stays on your phone and is automatically deleted after about 7 days.
  • From the analysis we upload still keyframe images (short bursts of frames around moments the app flags) and pose data (numeric body-landmark positions) to our backend, so your history and coaching are available across sessions.

Analysis and app activity

We store the results of each round's analysis (detected technique issues, coaching notes, your chosen stance/style/school, session metadata) and basic usage events needed to run features such as your weekly AI-analysis allowance.

Website

If you join a waitlist we store the email address you enter. The site uses lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics for aggregate traffic; it does not build advertising profiles of you.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the core product — analyse your rounds and return coaching.
  • To keep your history, progress and profile in sync across sessions/devices.
  • To enforce fair-use limits (e.g. the free tier's weekly AI analyses).
  • To communicate with you about the beta and the Service.
  • To secure the Service and debug problems.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service:

  • Supabase — our backend (authentication, database and file storage). Your account, pose data, keyframe images and analyses are stored here.
  • AI model provider (Google — Gemini) — to generate your coaching feedback, the app sends the keyframe images and the analysis context to our AI provider. Handling is subject to that provider's terms. We send only what's needed to produce feedback.
  • Google Play / Apple — app distribution and, if you buy a subscription in future, payment processing.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

4. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app), on the basis of your consent (e.g. camera use and joining a waitlist, which you can withdraw), and for our legitimate interests in securing and improving the Service.

5. How long we keep it

  • Round videos: on your device only, deleted after ~7 days.
  • Account, pose data, keyframes and analyses: until you delete them or your account.
  • Waitlist emails: until you ask us to remove them or the beta ends.

6. Your rights and choices

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app (Profile → Account → “Delete account & data”), or by requesting deletion at this page. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export or object to processing of your data — contact us to exercise these. EEA/UK users may complain to their local data-protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

7. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your stored data is restricted by row-level security so that, in normal operation, only you (and our backend on your behalf) can read your rows.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
